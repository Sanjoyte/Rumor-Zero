SECTION 04 // EPISTEMIC DOCTRINE

The Three Epistemic Pillars

Philosophical architecture guarding against gamification and information degradation.
PILLAR I

“Colour is a verdict, never a decoration.”

Green, red, amber, and violet are strictly quarantined from platform chrome, branding, marketing flourishes, and navigational elements. When a user sees color, it signals solely that an empirical assertion has met the cryptographic bar for truth.

Strict Rule: Zero chromatic badges on profile metrics, upvotes, or notifications.
PILLAR II

“Opinions are visually silent.”

Subjective beliefs, artistic interpretations, moral arguments, and editorial commentary represent protected civic discourse. They receive zero chromatic treatment. The total absence of color is the platform’s unambiguous cue that subjective discourse is being read.

Strict Rule: Opinions are never run through truth scoring or assigned pseudo-percentages.
PILLAR III

“Every color ships with a redundant cue.”

Epistemic integrity mandates universal accessibility. No reader is required to decode an assertion via color alone. Every verdict status is hard-paired with a distinct semantic glyph, an explicit text pill, and exact monospace confidence numerals.

Strict Rule: WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast and triple-channel encoding (Color + Glyph + Text Label).
